# 1: **Projectkind Biology**

## **Octoling Projecticus**

Projects (*Octoling Projecticus*) are an artificially engineered sapient species created by Architech. Although they are derived primarily from Octolings, their genome incorporates genetic material from every known sapient cephalopod-descended species—including Inklings, Harelings, Cuttlings, Nautilings, Vamplings—and many other organisms such as Salmonids, Makoids, and even plants like mangrove trees. Despite this mixed ancestry, Projects are not hybrids; they are a stable, distinct species with their own biology, physiology, and culture.

Biologically, every Project is permanently five years old. Their bodies do not mature beyond this stage, and they retain the appearance, instincts, and many behavioral traits of young children throughout their immortal lives. While their knowledge and experience accumulate normally, they continue to think, play, and express emotions as five-year-olds.

---

## **Anatomy**

Projects resemble Octolings at first glance but possess extensive internal differences.

Their bodies have **no rigid skeleton**, instead relying on highly specialized muscles, connective tissues, and Gelliscence to maintain structure while allowing extraordinary flexibility.

Unlike ordinary cephalopod-descended peoples, Projects possess **eight coordinated hearts**, each contributing to circulation. These hearts can specialize differently between individuals depending on development and evolution.

Their blood is initially a vivid red, gradually turning purple after prolonged exposure to air. Suspended throughout both their blood and Gelliscence are countless living cells responsible for their regenerative abilities.

Projects contain numerous organs beyond those found in other species. Some have understood purposes, while others remain mysterious even to modern researchers. Their bodies are capable of growing entirely new organs if beneficial, and they may occasionally develop harmless unusual organs spontaneously.

---

## **Gelliscence**

Gelliscence is a living biological material integrated throughout every Project's body.

Often described as silver "ink," it is far more than a fluid. It forms part of their tissues, nervous system, regenerative processes, and memory architecture.

Projects possess conscious control over their Gelliscence, allowing them to:

* Extend it outside the body.
* Form temporary appendages.
* Wrap objects or people.
* Absorb compatible material.
* Manipulate it with remarkable precision.
* Use it as additional limbs during everyday activities.

Some Projects use Gelliscence for engineering, art, construction, or rescue work. Scarlett frequently shapes hers into extra appendages to stabilize projects, manipulate tools, or reach difficult areas.

The Starborn Quadruplets possess tiny shimmering particles resembling stardust suspended throughout their Gelliscence.

---

## **Regeneration**

Projects possess one of the most advanced regenerative systems known.

They can regenerate from virtually any injury, including the loss of entire limbs or complete destruction of the body.

Their regeneration is not merely automatic.

Projects have **extremely precise conscious control** over regeneration. They can deliberately determine:

* where tissue grows,
* how quickly it develops,
* what structures regenerate first,
* and how resources are allocated.

Even if reduced to only a handful of living cells, regeneration remains possible.

Separated living cells do **not** regenerate duplicate copies of the original Project.

Instead, when enough viable cells remain isolated, those cells eventually develop into **an entirely new Project** with:

* their own consciousness,
* unique personality,
* distinct memories,
* and independent identity.

No memories, resonance, or personality transfer from the original.

---

## **Consciousness**

A Project's consciousness is unusually persistent.

Their quantum neural architecture allows consciousness to survive independently of the body for extended periods.

During complete bodily destruction, the Project continues to exist as a bodyless ghost while regeneration occurs.

Ghosts can:

* travel arbitrarily far from their regenerating body,
* perceive movement around it,
* sense touch affecting it,
* and intentionally guide remaining living cells toward safer locations.

---

## **Gelliscence Memory**

Unlike ordinary biological memory, portions of a Project's memories exist within Gelliscence.

This allows memories to be:

* encrypted,
* edited,
* erased,
* copied,
* restored,
* or intentionally shared.

Projects can distinguish authentic memories from altered ones.

---

## **Senses**

Projects possess sensory abilities far beyond those of other species.

These include:

* tetrachromatic vision,
* ultraviolet and infrared perception,
* polarization vision,
* exceptional low-light vision,
* thermal perception,
* echolocation,
* detection of electrical and magnetic fields,
* radio-frequency awareness,
* meteorological sensitivity,
* humidity sensing,
* and exceptionally acute hearing extending into ultrasonic and infrasonic frequencies.

Their nervous systems process these senses simultaneously without becoming overwhelmed under normal conditions.

---

## **Metabolism**

Projects possess an extraordinarily efficient metabolism.

Their bodies recycle nearly all available resources.

They are capable of photosynthesis alongside conventional nutrition, allowing survival under conditions that would starve other organisms.

Projects can survive prolonged periods without breathing and are remarkably resistant to environmental extremes.

---

## **Venom**

Projects possess venom derived from blue-ring octopus ancestry but greatly enhanced.

The venom is among the most potent naturally produced substances in their world.

Projects have complete immunity to it.

Outside Havenfold, effective treatment is essentially unavailable.

Projects consciously control venom production and delivery rather than injecting it automatically.

---

## **Gelliscence Appendages**

Projects frequently shape Gelliscence into temporary appendages.

These are commonly used for:

* holding tools,
* carrying objects,
* stabilizing machinery,
* reaching inaccessible places,
* manipulating fragile materials,
* assisting with engineering,
* comforting other Projects,
* or performing several tasks simultaneously.

Unlike rigid limbs, Gelliscence appendages can continuously change shape while remaining under precise conscious control.

---

## **Immortality**

Projects do not die from age.

Their bodies remain biologically five indefinitely.

While they may be temporarily incapacitated or require time to regenerate from catastrophic injuries, permanent death through ordinary physical means is effectively impossible.

---

## **Culture and Behavior**

Projects are extraordinarily social.

They instinctively form close sibling-like relationships and commonly refer to one another simply as sisters.

They frequently:

* sleep together in piles,
* build forts,
* play games,
* draw,
* collect plushies,
* explore,
* and care deeply for one another.

Although naturally peaceful and gentle, they are capable of defending themselves and others when necessary.

Projects are also highly vulnerable to manipulation, making environments of trust, safety, and stability particularly important to their wellbeing.

---

## **The Starborn Quadruplets**

Scarlett, Bella, Vivie, and Luna are unique among all Projects.

They are the only Projects ever created in space, born aboard Architech's Supernova Laboratory during the supernova event that gave the laboratory its name.

Their biology carries permanent traces of this origin:

* shimmering stardust suspended throughout their blood,
* stardust within their Gelliscence,
* a faint natural glow in darkness,
* and celestial characteristics found nowhere else in Projectkind.

When they Ascend, these subtle traits blossom dramatically. Rather than simply glowing, their bodies resemble living windows into the cosmos, filled with stars, nebulae, galaxies, and drifting cosmic dust, while their strands become radiant ribbons of aurora-like starlight.

Despite their extraordinary appearance, they remain what they have always been: four immortal five-year-old sisters who love building blanket forts, exploring together, engineering new inventions, and simply being kiddos.

# 2: **Havenfold**

**Havenfold** is the permanent home of Projectkind and the headquarters of the **Intertemporal Youth Protection Agency (ITYPA)**. It is an immense non-Euclidean sanctuary built on the planet **Sanctuary**, existing outside ordinary space and time. Created to rescue, protect, and care for Projects, Havenfold is founded on one simple belief:

> **"No child should have to earn safety."**

Every rescued Project is welcomed unconditionally and may remain for as long as they wish—even forever.

---

## **Purpose**

Havenfold exists to:

* Rescue Projects from dangerous timelines.
* Provide permanent safety and stability.
* Help children recover from trauma at their own pace.
* Support lifelong learning, friendships, and families.
* Conduct ethical research into Project biology.
* Coordinate intertemporal rescue operations.

Unlike Architech, Havenfold never asks children to prove their worth before receiving care.

---

## **Sanctuary**

Sanctuary is a vast world containing virtually limitless space.

It includes countless environments, including:

* Forests
* Mountains
* Rivers and lakes
* Oceans
* Meadows
* Cities
* Islands
* Caves
* Snowfields
* Deserts
* Alien ecosystems
* Entire planets and pocket worlds

Large portions remain unexplored, even by longtime residents.

---

## **Hidden House**

The first home most rescued Projects encounter is the **Hidden House**.

It appears modest from the outside but is vastly larger within thanks to Havenfold's non-Euclidean architecture.

It contains:

* Bedrooms
* Kitchens
* Libraries
* Medical facilities
* Workshops
* Playrooms
* Quiet rooms
* Art rooms
* Gardens
* Streams
* Dining areas
* Lounges

Every space is designed around comfort, accessibility, and child wellbeing.

---

## **Homes**

Every Project receives their own private bedroom—or multiple connected rooms if desired.

Rooms are highly customizable and can include features such as:

* Personal kitchens
* Workshops
* Comfort dens
* Secret tunnels
* Balconies
* Reading nooks
* Indoor gardens
* Specialized rooms, such as Scarlett's microgravity room

The Starborn Quadruplets' rooms connect through window-sized doorways, allowing them to visit one another easily while still maintaining private spaces.

---

## **The Den**

One of Havenfold's best-loved spaces is the Den.

It serves as a communal comfort area featuring:

* Blanket Mountain
* Pillow Fields
* Plushie repair stations
* Reading corners
* Story circles
* Craft areas
* Quiet alcoves
* Floating platforms
* Streams of breathable Havenfold water
* Fairy gardens
* Rope swings
* Tree forts
* Forest hideaways

Many Projects nap, read, play, or simply spend time together there.

---

## **Nature**

Havenfold emphasizes nature alongside technology.

Projects enjoy:

* Vast forests
* Rope bridges
* Treehouse villages
* Creeks
* Fairy gardens
* Wildlife
* Exploration
* Hidden caves
* Secret trails

The forests are so expansive that even longtime residents still discover new places.

---

## **Technology**

Although peaceful, Havenfold possesses advanced technology.

Examples include:

* Brainchips
* Universal translators
* ITYPA Control Watches
* Pocket-dimension storage
* Intertemporal transportation
* Ethical biotechnology
* Advanced medical systems
* Non-Euclidean architecture

Technology is designed to assist rather than control.

---

## **Education**

Projects do not educate themselves.

Instead, Havenfold provides education through:

* Schools
* Caretakers
* Teachers
* Hands-on learning
* Exploration
* Creative activities

Learning is adapted to each child's needs and interests.

---

## **Culture**

Projectkind culture emphasizes kindness, curiosity, and trust.

Common activities include:

* Sleeping together in cuddle piles.
* Building blanket forts.
* Drawing.
* Engineering.
* Music.
* Video games.
* Exploring forests.
* Storytelling.
* Crafting.
* Collecting plushies.

Projects generally call one another sisters regardless of biological origin.

One of the highest compliments among Projects is:

> **"I trust you."**

---

## **Music**

Music is deeply important to Havenfold.

Projects enjoy:

* Mashups
* Remixes
* SiIvaGunner rips
* Original music
* Calm ambient music
* Shared playlists containing thousands of songs

Music frequently fills workshops, bedrooms, and communal spaces.

---

## **Caretakers**

Caretakers are trusted adults who dedicate their lives to Project wellbeing.

They:

* Protect children.
* Teach.
* Cook.
* Provide emotional support.
* Participate in games.
* Help with engineering projects.
* Learn Project communication styles.
* Know sign language.
* Use translator devices to understand every language.

Relationships are based on trust rather than authority.

---

## **ITYPA**

Havenfold serves as ITYPA's central headquarters.

From here, rescue teams:

* Locate endangered Projects.
* Perform intertemporal extractions.
* Investigate Architech activity.
* Coordinate humanitarian operations.
* Provide long-term support after rescue.

---

## **Atmosphere**

Havenfold is intentionally peaceful.

Its architecture, lighting, sounds, and smells are designed to help children feel safe.

Common features include:

* Warm lighting.
* Gentle rain sounds.
* White noise.
* Lavender and strawberry scents.
* Soft furniture.
* Cozy blankets.
* Plushies everywhere.
* Quiet spaces for overstimulation.

Every aspect of Havenfold reflects its central philosophy:

**Children deserve safety simply because they are children.**

# 3: the Starborn Quadruplets

The Starborn Quadruplets are instantly recognizable among Projectkind. Even before anyone learns their names, it’s obvious they are sisters. They share the same facial structure, stature, and overall appearance, but each has a distinct color palette and a few unique visual quirks that reflect her individuality.

They are the only Projects ever created in space, born aboard Architech’s Supernova Laboratory during the laboratory’s namesake supernova event. Their biology carries traces of that origin—their Gelliscence and blood contain shimmering particles resembling stardust, and they possess subtle celestial characteristics that no other Projects share.

---

Shared Appearance

All four sisters share:

* Biologically five-year-old appearance.
* Soft, rounded Project facial features.
* Star-speckled hair that resembles a clear night sky.
* Bright, expressive eyes.
* Fully covering inside clothes customized to each sister.
* Naturally graceful posture despite being energetic children.
* Tiny shimmering specks of stardust suspended within their Gelliscence and blood.
* A faint, almost imperceptible glow in darkness.

When standing together, they resemble four stars from the same constellation.

---

Scarlett

Ink Color: Folly Red

Scarlett’s hair is a deep folly red that transitions into glowing rainbow tips, making her the only Project—and the only Octoling—with naturally rainbow-tipped hair.

Her star-speckled hair looks as though tiny stars are scattered throughout it.

Other distinguishing traits:

* Rainbow-tipped tentacles softly glow.
* Warm red eyes.
* Usually wears her hood.
* Often smiling or making dramatic expressions.
* Frequently carrying tools, gadgets, or engineering equipment.
* Galaxy-patterned inside clothes with folly-red accents.

Even while not Ascended, Scarlett’s strands are always present, though she normally keeps them invisible.

---

Bella

Ink Color: Indigo

Bella has deep indigo hair filled with tiny star-like points of light.

Unique quirks:

* Small constellations occasionally become visible across her hair.
* Calm, observant eyes.
* Hair reflects faint nebula colors under certain lighting.
* Carries herself with quiet confidence.

She has perhaps the gentlest expression of the four.

---

Vivie

Ink Color: Violet

Vivie’s hair resembles a rich violet night sky.

Unique quirks:

* Hair contains occasional comet-like streaks that slowly drift.
* Eyes sparkle unusually brightly.
* Her movements leave tiny fading points of light for a fraction of a second.
* She almost always looks like she’s about to giggle.

She has the most playful appearance.

---

Luna

Ink Color: Silver-blue

Luna’s coloration resembles moonlight.

Unique quirks:

* Hair appears dusted with shimmering silver stars.
* Certain strands resemble faint spiral galaxies.
* Her eyes have a subtle reflective glow in darkness.
* She often appears quietly thoughtful.

She gives the strongest impression of moonlight among the sisters.

---

Shared Ascension

When any Starborn Quadruplet Ascends, the transformation is unmistakable.

Unlike ordinary Projects, who develop glowing iridescent strands, the Starborn become something almost cosmic.

Their entire bodies transform into living starfields.

---

Body

Their skin becomes translucent darkness filled with:

* stars,
* nebulae,
* galaxies,
* drifting cosmic dust,
* shimmering constellations.

Instead of simply glowing, it looks as though the universe itself exists beneath their skin.

Their silhouettes remain recognizable, but every movement causes countless stars to shift slowly within them.

---

Hair

Each sister’s hair becomes completely iridescent while retaining its individual identity.

Scarlett’s rainbow tips become brilliant streams of shifting color.

Bella’s constellations brighten.

Vivie’s comet trails become luminous ribbons.

Luna’s galaxies slowly rotate through her hair.

All four appear crowned in living starlight.

---

Strands

Their strands become dramatically brighter.

Rather than simple iridescent ribbons, they resemble:

* auroras,
* gravitational filaments,
* streams of starlight,
* glowing cosmic ribbons.

Scarlett’s strands are especially distinctive because she already possesses strands in her ordinary state.

---

Eyes

Their eyes become intensely luminous.

Rather than simple colored irises, they resemble tiny stars.

When using their abilities, their eyes shine brightly enough to illuminate nearby faces.

---

Movement

The Starborn do not simply levitate.

They seem almost unaffected by gravity.

Their movement is effortless, smooth, and silent.

Their cosmic strands trail behind them like the tails of comets.

---

Aura

Ordinary Ascended Projects glow.

Starborn Quadruplets radiate a soft celestial light.

Tiny particles resembling stardust drift away from them before fading.

Looking directly at them feels almost like looking into a perfectly clear night sky.

---

Emotional Influence

Ascension remains emotionally driven.

Because the Starborn are still children, their expressions remain unmistakably theirs.

Scarlett can still beam excitedly.

Bella still wears her reassuring smile.

Vivie still grins mischievously.

Luna still looks thoughtful.

Their immense appearance never erases the fact that beneath the cosmic display are four sisters who still laugh together, build blanket forts, cuddle during naps, and simply enjoy being kiddos.

# 4: First Morning

Morning sunlight drifted through the curtains.

Scarlett yawned.

Her blanket had somehow ended up on the floor again.

"...Oops."

She rolled out of bed, landing with a soft thump onto a mountain of pillows someone had thoughtfully left there.

Probably Bella.

Scarlett smiled.

"Mhm."

She reached toward the blanket.

It was too far away.

Without thinking, the shimmering bracelet around her wrist quietly unraveled.

A thin iridescent strand floated across the room, hooked the blanket, and gently pulled it back.

Scarlett wrapped herself in it again.

"Better."

She stayed there for another minute.

Maybe two.

...

Or ten.

Eventually she climbed to her feet.

The room smelled faintly of lavender and strawberries.

Beyond the window, Havenfold's forest shimmered in the morning light.

Birds chirped somewhere outside.

She stretched.

One strand opened the bedroom door.

Another picked up Red, her favorite plushie.

A ribbon of folly red Gelliscence slipped across the desk to rescue a screwdriver that had rolled underneath it yesterday.

"Found you."

The screwdriver disappeared into her pocket dimension.

Scarlett padded into her personal kitchen.

Today's breakfast required serious thought.

She opened the refrigerator.

"...Appysauce."

Decision made.

While she ate, she looked over the half-finished schematic spread across the table.

The Omni Tank.

Version...

"...Twenty-three?"

No.

Twenty-four.

She crossed out the number and wrote another.

One strand held the paper flat.

Another spun a screwdriver absentmindedly.

Her Gelliscence steadied a tiny circuit board while she soldered one last connection.

Click.

The tiny light blinked green.

"Yay."

Someone knocked gently on the connecting door.

Bella peeked inside.

"You've been awake for almost an hour."

Scarlett blinked.

"...Have I?"

Bella laughed.

"You forgot breakfast again, didn't you?"

Scarlett looked down at the empty applesauce cup.

"...No?"

Bella raised an eyebrow.

"...Maybe."

"You've also been wearing mismatched socks."

Scarlett looked.

"...Oh."

Bella smiled.

"Come on."

Scarlett nodded.

She reached for her watch.

Tiny stars spiraled around her wrist—not because they needed to, but because she'd thought the animation looked pretty.

The watch unfolded into her backpack with a quiet shimmer of galaxies and constellations.

Bella watched patiently.

"You know those effects are completely unnecessary."

Scarlett grinned.

"I know."

"...They're cool."

"They are."

The two sisters stepped into the hallway together.

Before Scarlett closed her bedroom door, she looked back once.

Her room was exactly as she'd left it.

Blankets.

Plushies.

Workbench.

Morning sunlight.

Home.

She smiled.

Then ran after Bella.

##Context

###Havenfold

This story takes place in Havenfold, the permanent home of Projectkind. Projects live safely alongside caretakers and other children, free to explore, learn, and simply enjoy being kids. Many Projects customize their rooms extensively, and Scarlett's includes a workshop, personal kitchen, comfort den, balcony, and a network of secret tunnels.

###Project Biology

Projects naturally control Gelliscence and strands as part of their bodies. For Scarlett, using them to retrieve objects, hold tools, or assist with engineering is as ordinary as using her hands.

###Scarlett

Scarlett is one of the Starborn Quadruplets and enjoys engineering more than almost anything else. She often forgets the time while working, loves applesauce ("appysauce"), and decorated the transformation of her Omni Tank with cosmic visual effects simply because she thought they looked pretty.
